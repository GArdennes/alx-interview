## [Ugly Number II]()

An ugly number is a positive integer whose prime factors are limited to 2, 3 and 5.

Given an integer n, return the n<sup>th</sup> ugly number.

**Example 1:**
> **Input:**
n = 10

> **Output:**
12

> **Explantion:**

[1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 12] is the sequence of the first 10 ugly numbers.

**Example 1:**
> **Input:**
n = 1

> **Output:**
1

> **Explantion:**

1 has no prime factors, therefore all of its prime factors are limited to 2, 3, and 5.

**Constraints:**
* 1 $\leq$ n $\geq$ 1690
